# Critical Friend brief pre-mortem

Use this optional, read-only review after the Architect writes the Executor brief
and before dispatch. Skip it only for a genuinely trivial ticket and record that
choice on the ticket.

Read the ticket, brief, project instructions and project-owned lessons file. Assume
the Executor follows the brief and the independent Reviewer later finds a defect.
List the most likely causes, ranked Blocker, Major or Minor. Check:

1. vague or conflicting acceptance criteria;
2. missing failure, overlap or immediate-completion cases;
3. scope or dependencies belonging to another ticket;
4. missing verification or limitations;
5. conflict with source ownership, frozen-target or independence rules; and
6. whether the brief applies each relevant project lesson.

Label facts and judgements, cite the relevant file or section, and propose exact
wording changes. The Architect records adopted findings in the brief and ticket;
the Critical Friend has no veto or acceptance authority. Write only the external
brief-review artifact and finish with the agreed completion marker.
